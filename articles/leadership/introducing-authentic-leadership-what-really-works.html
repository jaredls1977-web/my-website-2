Introducing My New Book: "Authentic Leadership – What Really Works"

After years of watching leadership theory fail to survive contact with the real world, I wrote down what actually works instead.

By Jared Scott

After years of leading teams, navigating organizational challenges, and observing the disconnect between leadership theory and practice, I’m excited to announce the release of my book, Authentic Leadership – What Really Works. This is a book for leaders who are tired of hearing the same overused concepts and ready to cut through the noise to focus on what truly drives success.

Too often, leadership books and seminars make promises that don’t materialize in the real world. They present inspiring ideas but leave out the accountability and practical steps necessary for lasting impact. I’ve read countless leadership materials and attended my share of seminars, and I kept coming away with one thought: something is missing. Companies often say they believe in leadership, but do they really walk the talk?

In this book, I share my experiences—both successes and failures—providing insights that resonate with the everyday leader. From managing teams to overcoming organizational roadblocks, I offer an honest, practical approach to leadership that goes beyond buzzwords. You won’t find abstract theories here, but real-world examples, actionable steps, and anecdotes that show what happens when leadership fails and, more importantly, what happens when it works.

For leaders, managers, and anyone interested in learning how to truly influence and inspire others, Authentic Leadership – What Really Works is a guide that connects the dots between theory and reality. It’s time we shift the focus from simply talking about leadership to truly living it.

I’m grateful for the support of my colleagues, mentors, and everyone who has inspired the pages of this book. Let’s lead with authenticity—because that’s what really works.
