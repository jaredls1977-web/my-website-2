HOW TO ADD A NEW ARTICLE
========================

1. Get the article written inside the template.
   Open the file  _templates/new-article.html  and give it to your AI with this message:

     "Write my article inside this template. Keep everything the same except:
      the title, the description line, the date line, the dek, and the words
      in the article. Do not change any links or the lines marked 'do not change'."

2. Save the finished file with a short name, all lowercase, dashes instead of
   spaces, ending in .html. Example:  why-meetings-fail.html

3. On GitHub, open the folder for the topic:
      articles  >  leadership   (or faith, or recovery)
   Click  Add file  >  Upload files, drag your file in, click  Commit changes.

4. Wait about one minute. The article is on the site and in the lists.
   (To watch it happen, click the "Actions" tab. A green check means it is live.)

If the article should go in a brand-new topic, make a new folder inside
"articles" with the topic name (example: "podcast"). A new tab appears by itself.

Pictures for an article go in  assets/article-images  and are used like this:
   <img src="../../assets/article-images/my-picture.jpg" alt="Describe the picture">


HIDING AN ARTICLE
-----------------
Add this line inside the <head> of the article and it stays off the lists:
   <meta name="site-list" content="hidden">


CHANGING AN ARTICLE'S DATE OR SUMMARY
-------------------------------------
Open articles.json on GitHub, click the pencil, change the words, commit.


CHANGING THE MENU (top bar and footer)
--------------------------------------
Open  assets/js/site.js  and edit the list called NAV near the top.
One change updates every page.


ADDING A BOOK
-------------
Upload the cover to assets/books, then add a block to books.json
(copy an existing one and change the words).
